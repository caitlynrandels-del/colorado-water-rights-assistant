About This Tool

This research assistant gives Colorado water rights professionals a clean, fast interface to the public Division of Water Resources (DWR) HydroBase data via the official CDSS REST API.

It is designed for quick lookups of decreed rights (priority dates, amounts, case numbers, structure details) and current administrative calls. It is a convenience layer — not a replacement for official records, water court decrees, or professional judgment.
